size/history record for anything else.

  Ported onto 6.18 kernel APIs (timer_setup/timer_container_of, the DMA API, removed
  proc/set_fs/access_ok shims, macro-collision fixes) on top of the verbatim carry-forward
  from `main`. Compiles and links clean (zero errors, zero undefined symbols), loads clean
  on real hardware, and — after a class of timer-callback ABI bugs was found and fixed —
  beacons an AP that a real client associates to, gets DHCP from, and passes traffic over
  (22.6 Mbit/s iperf3, 0 retransmits), simultaneously with the 5 GHz radio, with zero OOM
  kills. Re-verified 2026-09-04: same image found already at 3h20m continuous uptime with
  both radios still up, closing the earlier "is long-run stability real" open question.
  Shipped in the release `seed.config` (`CONFIG_PACKAGE_kmod-rtl8192cd=y`, added only after
  this was proven on hardware); left out of `seed-min.config`, which stays wireless-free to
  fit a RAM boot. Same redistribution basis as `main` (see root README, *Credits &
  license*): Realtek copyright notices preserved intact; the core driver + phydm carry
  explicit GPLv2 grant headers; one chip-firmware blob
  (rtl8192cd/WlanHAL/Data/8197F/rtl8197Ffw.bin).
