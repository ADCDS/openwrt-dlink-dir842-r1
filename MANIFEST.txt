KEY FILES
---------
  rtl819x-eth.c        NIC + trunk bring-up. CPU-tag mode, the loader-exact RGMII
                       replica, and CPUICR1 CF_NIC_LITTLE_ENDIAN (without which a
                       NOR boot DMAs every frame byte-swapped and the wired
                       datapath is silently dead).
  rtl865x_asichal.c    clean re-implementation of the ASIC table engine
                       (netif/route/nexthop/ARP/L2/NAPT/extIP).
  rtl819x_hwnat.c      conntrack -> ASIC NAPT rows via ndo_flow_offload. The ASIC
                       keys and hashes on NUMERIC (host-order) values.
  rtl8367b.c           RTL8367S driver + rtl8367s_cpu_tag_enable() (reg 0x121a).
  pci-realtek.c        the PCIe fix that makes RTL8197F + RTL8822BE work at all.
  rtl8192cd.sh         netifd handler for the vendor 2.4 GHz driver. The
                       init_wireless_driver "$@" call is mandatory and its
                       absence is completely silent.
  09_wireless-dualband-dir842   seeds the vendor 2.4 GHz wifi-device
  10_wireless-5g-ifname-dir842  pins the mac80211 ifname to wlan1 so the 5 GHz
                       radio stops colliding with the vendor driver's wlan0
